Structural Principles

The design rules behind the architecture.

Data first, interpretation second
Each chapter opens with a data anchor block — the key numbers presented together, before any editorial. Research publication, not thought piece.
One climax per chapter
Each chapter has one argument at its peak. Ch4 peaks on the skills deadline; AI and 2030 vision serve it.
Maturity map from Ch2 onward
The 29/43/23/5 distribution introduced as a named framework in Ch2. Reader can place themselves. Benchmark quiz feeds into it.
Explicit chapter bridges
Each transition has a bridging paragraph. Ch2→3 specifically strengthened to move from system-level to human-level.
Trust the data to land
Research register, not keynote register. Where the numbers are alarming, the editorial steps back. Punchlines reserved for where data alone doesn’t convey the implication.
No Studytube in the narrative
No “organizations we work with” in analytical chapters. Credibility depends on the reader trusting we’re presenting research, not making a case.
Warm bridge to advice
A transitional sentence before each “What to do about it” section. Smooths the register change from analytical to prescriptive.
Pattern, not chain
The conclusion frames 4 chapters as a self-reinforcing pattern. Different organizations enter at different points. The benchmark shows yours.

AI Co-Reader — How It Works

Dynamic, not static. Every reader gets a personalized prompt based on what they’ve done in the report.

The AI Co-Reader builds prompts at click time from 2 data sources:

Data SourceWhat it adds to the promptWhen available
Role Selection “I am in the boardroom / I run L&D / I manage a team. Tailor to my perspective.” After selecting role in the summary
Benchmark Scores All 4 self-assessment answers with market averages and maturity level After completing the 4-question quiz

Prompt structure:

  1. Chapter title + source (Bilendi panel, sample sizes)
  2. Market data (the actual stats from that chapter)
  3. Key insight (the narrative takeaway)
  4. Personal context (role + benchmark scores + culture radar)
  5. Specific instruction (compare my scores, identify 3 biggest gaps, 90-day action plan)

If no self-assessment is completed, the prompt says: “Ask me about my industry, company size, and role to personalize your analysis.”

Three output channels:

  • ChatGPT — compact prompt, opens chatgpt.com/?q=... as URL (length-aware)
  • Claude — full markdown prompt copied to clipboard, opens claude.ai/new
  • Copy for agent — full markdown to clipboard for any AI tool
